Vardão Máquinas website

The institutional website of the company where I work, Vardão Máquinas, an authorized Husqvarna dealer and service center in Limeira, Brazil. It presents the company, the workshop and the product lines so customers can find the company on Google and take the conversation straight to WhatsApp. It complements Vardão Central, which serves the internal operation.

The problem

Vardão is a Gold-level Husqvarna dealer, with its own workshop and genuine parts at the same address. But people who want a chainsaw, a lawn tractor or a repair search on Google before going to the store. A company without a complete website does not show up in that search, and visitors need to understand quickly who the company is, what it sells, whether it repairs what they already own and how to reach someone.

The solution

A complete institutional website: the company's story and numbers, the service workshop explained, the product lines, customer reviews and a contact option on every page. The pages are generated from typed data, with the right title, description and structured data for search, and a button that takes the visitor to a salesperson with the message already filled in.

What the site does

Presents the company

The About page tells who the company is: an authorized Husqvarna dealer at Gold level since 2020, with store, workshop and genuine parts at the same address, how Vardão works and how to get there.

Shows the service workshop

A page of its own for the authorized workshop: what the service covers, factory warranty preserved and a free quote approved before any work.

Presents the product lines

Garden, forestry, robotics, family farming and battery, in pages that show the recommended use and lead to the specialist, so customers do not have to hunt for a contact.

Gathers social proof

Public customer reviews from Google, for people who do not know the store yet to see what others say.

Helps customers choose

Comparison and buying-guide articles, search inside the site and guides to parts and accessories by model, so customers decide with confidence before talking to a salesperson.

WhatsApp with context

Pre-filled messages, depending on what the customer was looking at, so the salesperson joins the conversation already knowing the topic.

Decisions

Pages generated from data

Products, articles and the company pages live in typed TypeScript data files, and a single template page generates each route at build time. Adding a new product means filling in an object, and the sitemap, title, structured data and navigation follow on their own. The gain is consistency: no page depends on someone remembering to configure SEO.

WhatsApp with the question already asked

Every call to WhatsApp carries what the customer was looking at: the product, the category, the article or the service workshop. That replaces "hi, how are you?" with a conversation that already starts on the topic, and the salesperson knows which page the contact came from.

Cookies with explicit consent

The Google Analytics script loads on every visit, but with Google Consent Mode starting as denied: until the visitor accepts, analytics and advertising cookies are not stored. Accepting or declining only updates that consent. The Google map on the contact page only loads if the visitor has already accepted analytics cookies or clicks "Carregar o mapa" (load the map). Declining takes nothing away from the site, and the choice is saved in the browser and can be changed later from a link in the footer.

Security in the headers

The site sends a content security policy that restricts the allowed origins, plus protection against being embedded in other pages and against file-type sniffing. In development it is more permissive than in production.

The right part, or none

The parts and accessories guide follows the same principle as the counter in Vardão Central: suggest only when there is certainty. For tractors and zero-turn mowers, for example, matching is done only by model code, never by name similarity. I would rather show no accessory than the wrong one.
