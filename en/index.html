Vardão Central

checking The system of the store where I work, in daily use

The system of Vardão Máquinas, an authorized Husqvarna dealer in Limeira, for the counter, sales, finance and inventory.

The problem

To answer "which part is it and how much", the counter opened a PDF catalog, a price spreadsheet and an old ERP. A wrong code becomes a return, and finance lived in another system.

How I prove it

  • I migrated the old ERP with the numbers checked to the cent: 1,107 automatic comparisons, no difference above 1 cent.
  • Over 2,000 automated tests and 70 end-to-end browser tests.

What I built

  • Counter: search by code, part or model, exploded view and quotes by PDF and WhatsApp, never guessing a code.
  • Sales: a CRM with a deal board, customer ranking, cross-selling and reactivation.
  • Finance: income statement, cash flow, working capital and ABC analysis.
  • Inventory: special parts orders, with the deposit paid by the customer.

Technologies React · TypeScript · Node.js · PostgreSQL · Prisma · RabbitMQ · Playwright

Vardão Máquinas website

Public company website, in production

The company institutional website, built so customers find us on Google and move the conversation to a salesperson. While Vardão Central runs the operation from the inside, the website brings in and serves customers: a catalog of about 200 pages, optimized for search, with direct WhatsApp contact.

  • Complete design and development: 136 product and category pages, 21 accessory pages, 38 articles and the institutional pages.
  • Catalog by category with search, an accessories guide by model and a WhatsApp button on every product.
  • Sitemap, structured data for search, a news feed and a cookie notice that lets visitors decline without losing anything.
